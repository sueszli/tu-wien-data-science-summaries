# CiteQ: citation sentiment

A course project from Jimmy Lin's [CS 848: The Art and Science of Empirical Computer Science](https://uwaterloo.ca/computer-science/current-graduate-students/courses/current-course-offerings/fall-2023) at the University of Waterloo (Fall 2023).

CiteQ used Semantic Scholar citation contexts and compared language models for classifying citations as positive, neutral, negative, or lacking usable context. A small hand-labeled sample was used to evaluate the models. Citation sentiment is not a measure of research quality, and model errors and missing context limit conclusions about individual researchers.

This note does not republish the original site, paper, citation records, or author-level results.
