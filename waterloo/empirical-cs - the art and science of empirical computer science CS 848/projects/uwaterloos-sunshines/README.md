# Waterloo compensation and research metrics

A course project from Jimmy Lin's [CS 848: The Art and Science of Empirical Computer Science](https://uwaterloo.ca/computer-science/current-graduate-students/courses/current-course-offerings/fall-2023) at the University of Waterloo (Fall 2023).

The original analysis combined annual salary disclosures with bibliometric data to study how compensation and research indicators vary across roles and years. The main lessons were about joining records across sources, checking data coverage, and separating correlation from causation.

This note does not republish the original site, individual records, rankings, or report.
